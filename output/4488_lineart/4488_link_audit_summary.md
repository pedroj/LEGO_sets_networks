# 4488 link audit (LEGO 4488-1 Millennium Falcon, mini)

Kernel: 459 instance links. Checked: 98 links (88 kept from kernel, 10 added = false negatives).

Kernel links removed as false positives: 371 (of which 201 are candidate pairs failing the geometry rules; the rest are pairs more than 6 LDU apart).

Connected components: 1 (largest 66 of 66 parts). Isolated parts: 0.

Rules: stud engagement (stud insertion of 2-5.5 LDU along an axis with >=8 LDU footprint; flush face or side contact is not a joint); non-stud joints (pin, axle, hinge, bar...) when the boxes touch and the subtype matrix allows the joint.
